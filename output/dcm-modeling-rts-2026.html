DCM Modeling Project Presented on RTS

Dcm Modeling Project Participants

Mon 01 June 2026

DCM Modeling Project funded by the Science Fund of the Republic of Serbia has been presented in the scope of the educational-scientific programme of the national TV station Radio Televizija Srbije LinkedIn Link.

Team members from the University of Kragujevac presented the DCM Modeling project in a dedicated TV show dedicated to artificial intelligence entitled: "VI i mi (Veštačka inteligencija i mi) - Partnerstvo u istraživanjima"

DCM Modeling is an interdisciplinary bilateral project realized in a partnership between Serbia and Italy which connects the domains of medicine, engineering, modeling and artificial intelligence with the goal of diagnosing and treating degenerative cervical myelopathy.
