Call for Papers: AAI2026 – Join Us in Shaping the Future of Applied Artificial Intelligence!
We are excited to invite researchers, practitioners, and industry experts to submit their work to the Fifth Serbian International Conference on Applied Artificial Intelligence (AAI2026)!

Kragujevac, Serbia

May 20th – 21st, 2026

AAI2026 brings together the AI community to explore cutting-edge research, real-world applications, and emerging trends across the field of applied artificial intelligence. Whether your work focuses on machine learning, computer vision, NLP, healthcare AI, smart systems, or interdisciplinary innovations — this is the place to share your ideas and connect with peers.

Why submit?

Present your research to an international audience
Engage with leading experts from academia and industry
Be part of a growing AI community in Southeast Europe
Publish your work in conference proceedings

Submission details and deadlines are available here:

https://aai2026.kg.ac.rs/

We look forward to your contributions and to welcoming you at AAI2026!
